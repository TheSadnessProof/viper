## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Move Generation & King Safety**:
   - `getLegalMoves` in `chessLogic.ts:532` filters candidate moves generated by `generatePseudoLegalMoves:291` by simulating the move on a cloned board (`cloneBoard`) and validating `!isSquareAttacked(cloneBoard, kingSq, opponentColor)`.
   - Across 35,356 filtered move candidates and 1,514 randomly played plies in `stress_test.ts`, neither White nor Black ever left their king in check, walked into check, or captured an opponent king.
   - Attack vectors for all piece types (Pawn, Knight, Bishop, Rook, Queen, King) in `isSquareAttacked:111` correctly detect threats even through pins per FIDE Art 3.9.

2. **Castling & En Passant Edge Invariants**:
   - Castling correctly evaluates empty squares and king transit attacks (`chessLogic.ts:460-520`). Attacking transit squares c1/d1 prevents queenside castling, while attacking rook transit square b1 does not prevent castling, adhering strictly to FIDE rules.
   - Castling while in check is prevented via `!isSquareAttacked(board, 4, 'b')` / `!isSquareAttacked(board, 60, 'w')`.
   - The en passant horizontal pin trap (where `dxe6` removes two pawns along rank 4 exposing the king on h4 to a rook on a4) is strictly flagged as illegal.

3. **State Transitions & History Rollback Fidelity**:
   - In `undoMove:864`, state is restored via `fromFEN(lastEntry.fen)` with `history.slice(0, -1)`.
   - Across 1,934 deep undo rollbacks, every state matched the pre-move state object across board pieces, turn, castling rights, en passant targets, halfmove and fullmove counters, and terminal status flags.
   - Reaching ply 0 from a 200+ ply game restores the initial starting position with zero drift.

4. **FEN Idempotency**:
   - `toFEN` (`chessLogic.ts:882`) and `fromFEN` (`chessLogic.ts:930`) were tested on 1,934 positions and 16 castling permutations.
   - `toFEN(fromFEN(toFEN(state))) === toFEN(state)` holds 100% of the time.

5. **Terminal State Recognition**:
   - Checkmate precedence over the 50-move rule was verified: a checkmating move on ply 100 correctly triggers `isCheckmate: true` and `isDraw: false`.
   - Stalemate is detected when legal moves are 0 and king is not in check.
   - Insufficient material correctly handles K vs K, K+B vs K, K+N vs K, and K+B vs K+B on same-color squares.

## 3. Caveats

- Monte Carlo games with purely random moves rarely encounter natural checkmates because random moves wander pieces without goal-oriented coordination; checkmate detection was explicitly tested via Fool's Mate, Scholar's Mate, and the Opera Game scenarios.
- Milestone 2 components (`chessAi.ts`, `chessAi.worker.ts`, `chessAudio.ts`) and Milestone 3 UI components (`ChessArena.tsx`, `ChessBoardView.tsx`) were not part of this core engine challenge and remain to be reviewed in subsequent milestones.
